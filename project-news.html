Pipeline

RSS feedsNews APIs→Extract & format→Validate & quality checks→NLP + topic clustering→RAG summaries→Streamlit dashboard

What I built

  1. ETL pipeline.

    Designed an end-to-end pipeline to extract, format and consolidate structured and unstructured data from multiple sources (RSS feeds and APIs) for reporting.

  2. BI dashboard.

    Built a Streamlit-based dashboard with topic clustering and trend analysis to publish insights, backed by automated data validation and quality checks.

  3. AI summarisation.

    Delivered AI-powered summarisation to speed up decision-making from large volumes of data.
